Frequently asked questions

What is Khmer Law Code?

Khmer Law Code is a mobile app for reading, searching and sharing Khmer law. It brings the 5 main law codes of the Kingdom of Cambodia together in one app, on iPhone and Android.

Which Cambodian laws are included?

The Constitution (160 articles), the Criminal Code (672 articles), the Criminal Procedure Code (612 articles), the Civil Code (1,305 articles) and the Civil Procedure Code (588 articles), plus the original PDF of each code.

How do I find an article?

Type an article number or any keyword into the search box. Matches are highlighted, and you can tap a key legal term to see its definition instantly.

Can I read Cambodian law in English?

Yes. Switch to English in a tap to search and read articles in English.

Is this an official government app?

No. Khmer Law Code is an independent app. Legal content is sourced from publicly available materials for information and study only.

How do I subscribe?

Subscribe with your Visa or Mastercard, or a virtual card from your banking app. The Help page shows each step on iPhone and Android.

Where can I download it?

On the App Store for iPhone and on Google Play for Android.
